Open the six-week planWeek by week, salsa and bachata, the drills that ran

Roughly 45 days to the first social. Enough to close the gaps Cavallo exposed: the rockstep footwork, the reads that keep me from collapsing to my old default, the clarity-vs-aggression line, and the hand tosses that rolled me twice. The rule: 30-45 minutes a day, two partner sessions a week, hit a real floor whenever one exists. Foundation first, flash last.

WEEK 1Jul 20-26

Lock the fundamentals

Salsa
  • Reinforce what Cavallo cleaned
  • On1 timing, cross-body on the 1
Bachata
  • Keep the L3-5 base warm
  • Frame + weight transfers
Drill: 15 min metronome daily. Cross-body to 3 songs, zero timing slips. Rebuild the base before adding load.
WEEK 2Jul 27-Aug 2

Rockstep & cumbia-step footwork · leak 3.2

Salsa
  • Rockstep & cumbia step on switch-hand turns
  • Footwork into the hammerlock entry
Bachata
  • Dominican taps for foot speed
  • Syncopation carries over to salsa
Drill: Isolate the switch-hand-turn footwork. 20 reps slow, then at tempo, until the rockstep stops disappearing.
WEEK 3Aug 3-9

Reading the follow · leak 3.1

Salsa
  • Build 3 mid-range "probe" moves
  • Kill the default to the Fast-Track combo
Bachata
  • Same idea: read, then choose
  • Scale sensual moves to her level
Drill: Dance 5 follows of different levels in one night. Probe with mid-level moves. Never collapse to the old default.
WEEK 4Aug 10-16

Turns & toss-into-turn · leak 3.4

Salsa
  • Spotting + prep, clean turn leads
  • Toss-into-turn timing
Bachata
  • Lady's turn, controlled exit
  • Double turn without the wobble
Drill: 10 clean singles each side on relevé, then the toss-into-turn slow until the release and catch line up.
WEEK 5Aug 17-23

Clarity vs aggression · leak 3.3

Salsa
  • Frame, tension & compression
  • Find the line: clean, not soft
Bachata
  • Closed-position leads with intent
  • Redirects that read, not shove
Drill: 2-3 partner sessions. Every rep, ask for the note: too soft, just right, or too much. Dial in the middle.
WEEK 6Aug 24-30

Polish the hard combos · leak 3.4

Salsa
  • Bank last week's two new combos, keep them clean
  • Sharpen the hammerlock-to-cuddle chain (Mar 25)
Bachata
  • String a full combo, hit the mambo accents
  • Body roll to turn to dip, clean
Drill: Run the exact two combos that rolled me, at full song tempo, until they are boring. Then freestyle to unknown tracks.
PEAK WEEKAug 31-Sep 2

Sharpen, social, rest

Do
  • Two social nights, low pressure
  • Run the combos, keep them clean
Don't
  • No new material this week
  • Rest the legs, stretch, hydrate
Arrive fresh: confidence beats cramming. The reps are already in - land in Waikiki loose, warm, and ready to hunt songs.
